I am the maintainer of OpenStrap. Looking for designers who can help design this app, not actually help complete design system setup. I don't want you to design the entire application, all I want is a proper design system with icons, fonts, layouts, UI direction, and major screens like workout, home, vitals and all those screens, base properly built. Once that's done I can pick it up from there.

What do you get in return? I can't provide money for this, but I am open to asking the community for donations for this. While its success is uncertain, what I can guarantee is a proper shoutout on our Discord channel which has 200+ members, Reddit community, and proper mention and credits in the GitHub README. If you own an agency, would actually love to do a crossover post lol.

Being strict on what the requirements are, I don't want straightforward UI, the design you come up with should be very well thought through and backed by proper research.

Here's what the app is, shortly: a passionate fitness app with the motto to solve health, wellness, nutrition, workouts, all in a single app, with wearables being the major revolving point of the app. Currently supports WHOOP 4, 5, MG, generic health straps; support for Oura rings, Polar bands and a few other devices is in the pipeline.

Making this clear current ui was made heavily made using AI. Thanks.

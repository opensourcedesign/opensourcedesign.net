**About MineServe**
[MineServe](https://mineserve.app/) is a free, open-source (GPL-3.0) Android app that lets users host dedicated Minecraft servers (Paper, Purpur, Fabric, Forge, Bedrock, and Vanilla) natively on their Android phone or tablet without root, alongside an optional cloud portal ([cloud.mineserve.app](https://cloud.mineserve.app/)).

The current app icon and cover graphic were AI-generated placeholders. I am looking for a human graphic designer or illustrator (**strictly no generative AI**) to replace them with clean, original vector artwork.

**Visual Direction & Color Scheme**
- **Current vibe:** Dark, technical, Minecraft/server-inspired palette:
  - Deep Obsidian / Slate backgrounds: `#0B0F19`, `#0F172A`, `#1B2333`
  - Primary Emerald accents: `#10B981`, `#34D399`
  - Optional secondary accents (Diamond / Gold / Redstone): `#06B6D4`, `#F59E0B`, `#EF4444`
- **Open to fresh concepts:** I like the current voxel/server-block direction, just cleaner and less overly detailed so it stays crisp at small launcher icon sizes, and I am also open to a fresh concept if you have a strong idea.
- **Trademarks:** Blocky/voxel, server rack, or terminal motifs are welcome, while avoiding official Mojang/Minecraft logos or trademarked characters.

**Process & Budget**
- **Budget:** Around $200 USD (open to hearing your standard quote for the full package or icon-only).
- **Process:** Rough sketch / concept stage before final vector rendering, with 1 to 2 rounds of revisions. Milestone payment via buyer-protected invoice or PayPal Goods & Services.
- **Rights:** Full commercial/redistribution rights so final assets can be committed to the public GPL-3.0 GitHub repo and redistributed via F-Droid and Google Play.
- **Potential future work:** Also open to follow-up work on a broader MineServe UI refresh, branding for LinuxOnAndroid, or YouTube thumbnails if we work well together.

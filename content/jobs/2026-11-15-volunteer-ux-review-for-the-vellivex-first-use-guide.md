Vellivex publishes experimental GPL-3.0 privacy tools. We welcome volunteer usability reviewers to help make the public first-use guide easier to follow. Spend around 10 minutes on the guide. Locate VVault and read the save-and-reopen directions. Tell us where you first hesitated and suggest one clearer line of copy. The written guide is available without an account. Trying a hosted demo is optional and can require an email code. If you try it, use a fictional sample and leave passwords, keys and personal data out of your feedback.

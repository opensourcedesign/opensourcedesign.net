Open Networked Earth (O.N.E.) is an open-source, post-enclosure civilizational architecture designed to replace predatory debt and resource speculation with thermodynamic conservation and demarchic sortition.

Unlike theoretical proposals or crypto projects, the foundational groundwork is fully codified:
- An 11-chapter Living Constitution (dynamic usufruct & sortition lotteries).
- A thermodynamic Leontief input-output engine balancing real exergy (kWh) and dry mass (kg).
- A scientific discrete-event simulation engine in Julia (SIMONE).
- Physical open-hardware blueprints for islanded microgrids and modular CNC timber habitats (WikiHouse compatible).
- 6 completed novels and full audiobooks.

### The Operational Bottleneck & Inversion:
I can write the mathematical equations, maintain the Julia models, and draft constitutional mechanics, but I cannot simultaneously produce video edits, manage daily community triage, and run social channels.

We are seeking an active **Media, Storytelling & Video Co-Steward (Pillar 0)** to join as a peer foundational partner (Co-Founder tier).

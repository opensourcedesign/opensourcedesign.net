# Inactive job posting guide

An inactive job posting is inactive when it has gone without changes to it's posting for 3 months.

An **optional step** for community members is to reach out to inactive posts to update the details to indicate it is still actively looking for help or update the status to indicate if it is closed or filled.

Why?
- Great way for us to understand why job has not been updated
- Which projects are not being filled
- How we can fill a project and how we can help projects get the help they need

## Best practices
Want to help out? Consider only reaching out to projects who have a "May be inactive" tag.
- First, check the table below if they have already been contacted.
- If they **do not** reply, the job board will automatically archive jobs that are past their deadline or go on for a year without updates.
- If they **do** reply, this is a great opportunity to also look at their job posting to see how they can improve the description for designers to better understand what they need.

## Email template

This is the template I have used but you do not have to use this.

Minimum requirements to include is:
- Link to their job posting
- Why you are reaching out and who you are
- How to update their project status
- Feedback form link
```
Hello!

My name is Dana and I am one of the maintainers of the job posting board for the Open Source Design community.

I am reaching out because I noticed your job posting ["Title of their job posting here"](link to their job posting) is marked as inactive.

Would you like to keep this job open? If yes, please update the details [here](link to edit their job posting here). 
If not, please edit the job posting to the appropriate status.

Any feedback on how we can improve this experience for you would be greatly appreciated! (: Fill out the form [here](https://cloud.opensourcedesign.net/apps/forms/s/xAJzdpgds5Ri97rEi7dqaq73) or send it to me directly.

Thank you,
Dana (Community member of Open Source Design)
```

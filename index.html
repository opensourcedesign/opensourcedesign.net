A community of designers and developers

Bringing great design to open source software.

We push for open design processes and better user experience in open source, through a job board, events, and an open community.

Good design should be as open as good code.

We are a global community of designers, developers, and advocates improving the usability of open source software. We run a job board connecting designers with projects that need them, organize events and conference design tracks, and share openly licensed resources.

New here? Read about the community, our manifesto, and how to join. Conversations happen in the open, on our forum.
